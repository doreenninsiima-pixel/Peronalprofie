About Me

Who I Am

My name is Doreen Ninsiima, a hardworking and determined young woman with a deep passion for Information Technology. I am currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Business Information Technology (BIT) at Ndejje University.

Born and raised as a proud Munyakole from Mbarara, I carry with me the values of resilience, community, and continuous self-improvement. I love learning new things, sharpening my computer skills, and expanding my knowledge in the ever-evolving world of technology.

My Journey & Experience

Beyond the classroom, I currently work with the UPDF Engineering Brigade, where I get the opportunity to apply and grow my technical skills in a professional environment. This experience has strengthened my discipline, problem-solving ability, and commitment to excellence.

My Goal

My ultimate goal is to develop strong IT skills and continuously improve my technology expertise so that I can solve real problems in society. I believe technology has the power to transform communities, and I want to be part of that positive change — starting right here in Uganda.
